01

The problem

Operators had loads of data but it was hard to know what to actually do with it.

Their takings, customer data and competitor info all lived in different places, so nobody had the full picture. Dojo sees 49 million cardholders every month, so we had a real chance to pull it all together into one place operators would actually use.

02

What I did

I

Customer research

I spoke to hundreds of operators to understand what they really meant when they asked for “better reporting”.

II

Testing demand

Before we built anything, we undertook usability testing with customers, testing hooks and messages using emails and in-app cards.

III

Pricing

We used the Van Westendorp method to work out what operators would pay for and how we could bundle this alongside other products/tiers.

How operators used it
Diagnosis
Explaining a drop in footfall

One operator saw footfall drop 11% and worried it was something in the venue. The data showed it was actually a dip across the whole local area.

Loyalty
Growing loyal customers

Another spotted a group of regulars who were one or two visits away from becoming loyal customers and changed their loyalty thresholds to bring them back more often.

Campaigns
Filling quieter evenings

One group found they were losing evening trade to local competitors, so they ran a targeted campaign and measured it against a control group.
