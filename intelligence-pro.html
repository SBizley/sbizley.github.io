What operators see each week
01

The problem

Dashboards everywhere, answers nowhere.

Operators had takings in one system, customer data in another and no idea how they compared to the venue down the road. Dojo sees 49 million cardholders a month, so the bet was one view an operator would actually act on.

02

What I did

I

Talked to operators

Hundreds of customer interviews to work out what “I want better reporting” actually meant.

II

Tested before building

Fake doors, emails and in-app cards to check demand first, and only built what people clicked on.

III

Priced it on evidence

A Van Westendorp survey showed where a free feature stopped and a paid tier started.
