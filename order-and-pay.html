The problem

The experience ended the second a guest paid.

Restaurants were short-staffed, so nobody was asking guests for reviews or tips. The job was to grow revenue without slowing down a flow staff use mid-service.

02

What I did

I

Built the foundation

Launched Order & Pay and QR payments, and redesigned every screen from basket to receipt.

II

Tested what happens after paying

Quick experiments on review prompts, tipping and upsell, then scaled the winners.

III

Rolled it out

Took it live across our customers and 6+ EPOS partners, and kept iterating on their feedback.

03

The outcome

Within six months, merchant tips were up 140% and review conversion up 115%. Across the three squads, net revenue grew 163% year on year.
