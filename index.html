Hey, I'm Sophie.

Since 2017 I've worked across product, go-to-market, strategy and operations, mostly on 0-to-1 propositions. I love taking new concepts to market, especially working out willingness to pay and running small experiments before a launch.

At Dojo, I built Intelligence from a Looker dashboard into a multi-app, multi-market proposition, and built the remote payments products, which grew to £2.4B a year in transaction volume.

I build AI agents to automate the manual parts of my work, and I've made a few passion projects in Lovable.

How I take products to market.

Find out who'll pay

Before we built Dojo Intelligence, we tested hooks and messages with customers by email and in-app cards.

Get the price right

Van Westendorp research to price Intelligence and bundle it into tiers. At Waitrose, testing packages lifted enquiry conversion by 50%.

Sell through partners

At Dojo, payment links went out through our partner API, and Order & Pay went live with 6+ integration partners. At John Lewis, I worked with PayPal and buy now, pay later providers to get them into checkout.

Train the sales team

Trained 500+ sales reps and account managers on each new product, and fed what they heard back into the roadmap.
