Hey, I'm Sophie.

I've spent the last decade working across product, strategy, go-to-market and operations in fast-moving tech and ecom businesses. I'm happiest somewhere in the middle of it all: getting close to customers, working out what actually matters, and turning ideas into products people actually use.

I'm increasingly interested in what AI can make possible too: fewer pointless tasks, better ways of working, and giving small teams more leverage. I write about practical AI and the realities of working in tech on Substack.

I'm now looking for what's next: ideally a small team with a big problem, and people I can learn a lot from. Think Chief of Staff, operator or founding product roles at ambitious startups.
