How I like to work

I hate busywork, I love a good problem, and I'd rather test an idea than argue about it.

Automate the busywork

I built AI agents and Jira, Slack and Notion automations that took over 6 team workflows, from company updates to roadmap syncing and customer insight triage, then opened them up to PMs across Dojo. My squad got 5+ hours a week back, and the wider PM team about a day a week.

Get close to customers

I spend time with merchants and sales teams until I find the question behind the question. Then I dig into the jobs they're trying to get done, because that's where the growth is.

Experiment to conviction

Gut feel gets a hypothesis. Experimentation gets a decision. Fake door tests, A/B variants, usage signals. On a small team, rigour has to be there from day one.

Bring people with me

I've trained 500+ sales reps, run vision workshops with founders and presented strategy to the whole company. A plan only works if people believe in it.
