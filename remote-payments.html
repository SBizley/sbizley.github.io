01

The problem

Getting paid shouldn't need the customer at the counter.

Merchants were taking card details over the phone and keying them into the card machine. It was slow, fraud-prone and frustrating for their customers.

02

What I did

I

Picked the right first product

Payment links went first because they fixed the phone problem straight away. SDKs came later.

II

Made it feel safe

Unpaid-link alerts and branded receipts, so merchants trusted a new way of taking real money.

III

Said no a lot

Kept checkouts simple across four markets and only added payment methods that earned their place.

03

The outcome

A new line of business built from nothing, now used by some of the biggest UK hospitality groups. It won Team of the Year and a Creator Award, and became the base Dojo's hospitality products were built on.
