Venture 01 · Wine Tasting at Home
A guided wine tasting, delivered to your door.
Lots of people were interested, but enquiries weren't turning into bookings and we didn't know what to charge.
Venture 01

What I did

I

Finding the drop-off

I mapped the journey from enquiry to booking to see where people were dropping off.

II

Testing price and packages

We tried different tasting sizes, occasions and gift options until we found what people actually wanted to buy.

III

Training the experts

We trained Waitrose's own wine experts to run the tastings in people's homes.

Venture 01

The outcome

Enquiry conversion went up 50% and the tastings got 150+ five-star reviews on Trustpilot. It grew from a small trial into a national business.

Venture 02 · While You're Away
Groceries delivered, unpacked and put away while you're out.
An industry first that asked customers to let a retailer into their home.
Venture 02

What I did

I

Testing demand

We took it from an idea to a local beta to see whether people would really use it.

II

Building the tech

We worked with a security partner on smart locks and one-time access for drivers, built by an offshore engineering team I was responsible for.

III

Deciding what next

We looked at whether it could work at scale, and it couldn't.

Venture 02

The outcome

A few hundred customers used it and the interest was there. But pets, trust and the cost of having someone in your home outweighed the convenience, so it was pivoted before it was scaled across the country. Elements of it still exist today.
