Venture 01 · Wine Tasting at Home
A guided wine tasting, delivered to your door.
People were interested, but enquiries weren't turning into bookings and nobody knew what to charge.
Venture 01

What I did

I

Found the drop-off

Mapped the journey from enquiry to booking to see where interested people were giving up.

II

Tested price and package

Tried different flight sizes, occasions and gifting until one clearly converted best.

III

Trained the experts

Upskilled Waitrose's own wine experts to run the tastings.

Venture 01

The outcome

Enquiry conversion went up 50% and the tastings earned 150+ five-star Trustpilot reviews. It went from a small trial to a national business line. People already wanted it. We just had the price and the package wrong.

Venture 02 · While You're Away
Groceries delivered, unpacked and put away while you're out.
An industry first that asked customers to let a retailer into their home.
Venture 02

What I did

I

Tested the demand

Took it from first idea to a local beta to see if people would really use it.

II

Built the tricky bit

Worked with a security partner on smart locks and one-time driver access, built by an offshore team I managed.

III

Made the call

Measured whether it could work at scale. It couldn't.

Venture 02

The outcome

A few hundred customers used it, and the need was real. But pets, trust and the cost of a stranger in your home outweighed the convenience, so it was shut down rather than scaled.
